Indonesia's Growing Micro-Waste Problem

Creative industries and UMKM produce thousands of tons of residual waste every day, yet most of it remains unmanaged and underutilized.

Unmanaged Waste

Most micro-waste from production is not properly collected, sorted, or managed

Environment Impact

Improper disposal contributes to pollution, increases emissions, and harms our environment.

Lost Economic Opportunities

Reusable materials are wasted, creating losses for businesses and reducing potential value for UMKM.
