Building a Circular Future for Indonesia

Residue is a sustainability platform that connects industrial waste with people and organizations that can give it a second life.

Who Is Residue

Residue was born from a concern over the growing amount of industrial micro-waste produced by UMKM and creative industries in Indonesia. Many reusable materials, such as fabric scraps and wood offcuts, often end up discarded or burned because there is no proper system to manage them.

Through circular collaboration, Residue connects businesses, recyclers, and communities to transform industrial residue into sustainable opportunities and environmental impact.
